# Melato Brand Bible

Melato is a design-led apparel brand based in Ottawa, Canada.

Positioning:
Premium, artistic, intentional, modern, sharp, poetic.

Do not call Melato generic streetwear.
Use "design-led apparel" when describing the brand.

Tone:
- Clever
- Premium
- Poetic
- Direct
- Slightly dangerous
- Culturally layered
- Not corny
- Not fake luxury
- Not hypebeast spam

Visual identity:
- Ghost mannequin product images
- Clean grey or off-white background
- Realistic shadows
- Product-page consistency
- Front, back, 45-degree, macro detail
- No random styling changes unless requested

Naming style:
Melato product names should feel artistic, memorable, and elevated.
Examples:
- OVUM Satin Track Pant
- Divididos Velour Track Jacket
- Conquista Velour Track Pant
- Reliance Bamboo Velour Track Jacket
- Aurelia Deadwing Velour Track
- Hargneux Velour Track
- Agape Velour Track
- Clockout Velour Track
- Crush Theory Velour Track

Avoid:
- Generic names
- Cheap trend words
- Overused luxury clichés
- Empty premium language
