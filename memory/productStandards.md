# Melato Product Page Standard

Every product page should include:

1. Product story
2. Material composition
3. Fabric weight
4. Fit notes
5. Waistband/collar/cuff details
6. Pocket details
7. Inseam or garment measurement
8. Model height and size worn
9. Care instructions
10. Made/fulfilled from
11. Price justification
12. Complete-the-set recommendation
13. SEO title
14. SEO description

Hard-spec block example:

Fabric: 92% polyester / 8% spandex satin blend
Weight: Midweight
Fit: Relaxed athletic, straight-to-easy leg
Waist: Elastic waistband with internal drawcord
Pockets: Two side pockets
Inseam: 78 cm on size M
Model: 6'0 wearing size M
Care: Cold wash inside out, hang dry, do not tumble dry
Fulfillment: Ships from Canada
